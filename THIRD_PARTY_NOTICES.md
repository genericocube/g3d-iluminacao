# Third-party notices

## g3d

This project bundles the g3d engine from https://github.com/groverburger/g3d.
The files in `g3d/` are third-party software and retain their upstream
copyright headers. `g3d/camera.lua` and `g3d/objloader.lua` contain small bug fixes
(see the README). The upstream MIT license is included as `g3d-LICENSE`.

The demo's OBJ models, textures, WAV test sounds and the GLB model were created for these
projects by `generico_cube`; they are separate from the g3d engine.
